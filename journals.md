Entry 2 – 3D Modelling

Date: About 1 month ago
By: A. Adarsh

I worked on the complete 3D modelling of the robot dog. I finished almost all the modelling in one day and it took me around 8 hours. It was actually really fun and I enjoyed doing it.

I did all the modelling using Tinkercad. Some parts were difficult to make perfect, so I also used a few models from online as reference to improve accuracy, because my own design was not good enough in some areas.

Entry 3 – PCB Design

Time Spent: 6.0 Hours (actually more)
Date: About 1 month ago
By: A. Adarsh

Today I completed the PCB design for the robot dog. This was my first proper PCB design, so I spent a lot of time understanding routing and connections.

I created the schematic and PCB design. I spent more than 5 hours on this because I am still new to PCB designing. I faced many small mistakes and had to redo parts multiple times.

Earlier, I always used direct wire connections or breadboards, so this felt like a big step forward for me. I am really excited to get this PCB printed because it will be my first custom PCB.

Design files and images are added in the repository. I feel confident that in future I will become much better at PCB designing.
